## Rulings (all proposed, Vibhav to confirm)

- **O1, flag.** Off by default. Off: the four reads answer the non-retryable 503 `feature_unavailable`; the four POSTs answer 403 `feature_unavailable` (definite, so the screen never locks), nothing read or recorded.
- **O2, no email provider.** `outreach.connection` is always `{connected: false, sender: null, revision: 1}`. `outreach.connect` and `outreach.send` are refused with 403 `feature_unavailable`, `reason: 'email_sending_unsupported'`, before the body is read and before any ledger claim: no OAuth URL, no token, no attempt row, nothing transmitted. The screen shows its permission sentence and keeps the draft.
- **O3, contacts.** Official public offices only, from the static directory; never a person's address, never a guessed one. Emails are null except an organizational mailbox printed on the official page (NC State Parks). Landlord: none ("HALO does not look up landlords"). Representative: the two official address-based finders, because districts split counties. Utility (public water only): the state Public Water Supply Section, labelled as the regulator and not the person's utility. Local office: the county's local health department (from the NC DHHS directory; its own https site, or the directory page when the listed site is plain http), plus the state division for the topic, or the county Extension center for volunteering and other questions. A city or ZIP maps to the county containing its Census internal point, and the match reason says so. No area, or an id outside the gazetteer: no contacts and a sentence saying no official contact is known.
- **O4, public context.** Drinking water only: the county's latest U.S. Drought Monitor release, and for public water the NC DWR conservation status of each listed system in that county (B12's `ncWaterStewardship.json`, dated). Other topics: `empty`. `revision` is the water file's retrieval date as `yyyymmdd`.
- **O5, prepare builds a copy-only draft.** Exactly the screen's keys. The selected notes are read from the household's workspace at their exact revisions, and the public context entry from O4 at its revision; the server composes the included sections exactly as the screen does (`ProductionOutreach.tsx:43-44`) and the body must end with them (else 422). The area must be the saved Explore area (else 409). `senderRevision` must be 1. The answer is the prepared-message shape with `from: null` and `expiresAt: null`, `status: 'draft'`, `delivery: 'copy'`: it is never a send review, so the screen's `validOutreachReview` rejects it, which is correct.
- **O6, drafts and revisions.** One draft per household, recipient class and lowercased recipient address (an empty address is its own draft). A changed prepare raises the revision by one; an identical one keeps it. At most 100 drafts per household. The ledger receipt holds the draft id and revision only. A replay answers the draft if it is still at that revision, else 409 `changed_elsewhere`.
- **O7, history and status.** History: `{attempts: [], complete: true, drafts, unresolvedOperations}`; `attempts` is truthfully empty because nothing is ever sent. Status of a draft id is the draft with `status: 'draft'`; any other id is 404.
- **O8, retention and export.** Drafts are kept until clear all or account deletion; there is no delete route in the contract. Export: explore group, the recipient class, topic, water context, revision and dates in the base tier; name, address, subject, body, note and source snapshots and the area as private text. The flag stays off until this retention is confirmed.

## Dropped or blocked, and why

- Gmail OAuth, token storage, send proposals and delivery status: no provider is approved (O2).
- Server-side contact search or scraping: never; the directory is static and source-recorded (O3).
- Per-person staff emails: dropped even where a page prints one (O3).
- Draft deletion and disclosure of draft persistence on screen: no route or copy exists in the frontend; open for the frontend lane.
